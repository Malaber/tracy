Your time, entered simply.

Tracy Time Tracking brings quick time entry and recent-day review to iPhone, iPad, and the web.

A moment to enter. A moment to check.

Check in or out, correct times, add breaks and notes, and review missing or unfinished days. Statistics show recorded working time and daily targets.

Keep going offline

Sign in once while online. Entries save on your device, including offline corrections. Tracy retries sync when connectivity returns while the app runs, and when you reopen it. Conflicting changes are shown for your review.

Native and accessible

Standard iOS controls, Liquid Glass on supported systems, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, and system, light, or dark appearance. Supports iOS 17 and later.

Your account, your server

Use a passkey with tracy.malaber.de, or connect to your own compatible HTTPS Tracy server. The iOS app is being tested through TestFlight; public App Store availability is not yet announced.
